Our thesis

Boost success without breaking the IVF chain.

EpicOva is built as an add-on to the fertility clinic workflow. The aim is not to replace IVF — but to improve the biological quality of the starting material before fertilization.

🥚

Better eggs

More mature oocytes per cycle. The product thesis is to recover immature or unstable oocytes by improving cellular quality before IVF or ICSI.

⏱

Faster journey

More usable oocytes per retrieval may reduce the need for repeated stimulation cycles, shortening the total time to live birth.

💰

Lower cost per baby

The business logic targets time to baby, cost per baby, and life disruption — metrics that matter to patients, clinics and payers.

The IVF challenge

Over 95% of women seeking assisted reproductive technology opt for IVF — yet results deteriorate sharply with age.

IVF can retrieve, fertilize and transfer. It cannot, by itself, confer competence on an embryo.

The meaningful outcome is cumulative live birth per intention to treat, read together with risks, cost, time and patient experience. ForEve addresses the upstream biological constraint: the age-related decline in oocyte quality.

23.7% live births for women 38–40 · 7.6% for women over 40

Science

Targeting the root cause of egg aging.

ForEve's research links oocyte aging to heterochromatin loss, retrotransposon activation and DNA damage. The therapeutic logic is to restore chromatin regulation, repress retrotransposons and improve oocyte function.

01

Mechanism

With age, chromatin becomes less heterochromatic. This loss of compaction disrupts transcriptional regulation in the oocyte, impairing its developmental competence.

02

Damage pathway

Retrotransposon activity increases in aged oocytes and contributes to DNA damage, loss of chromosomal stability and progressive cellular dysfunction.

03

Recovery strategy

ForEve's preclinical work aims to enhance heterochromatin compaction and reduce retrotransposon expression in aged oocytes, measured by validated biological markers.

Platform

From EpicOva to an epigenetic cell rejuvenation platform.

The first program targets oocytes because the unmet need is clear, measurable and deeply human. The broader platform vision extends to T cell augmentation, tissue transplantation and ocular dystrophy after validation of the core rejuvenation logic.

01

Restore heterochromatin

Target epigenetic modifiers to rebuild chromatin organization in aged oocytes and recover their transcriptional stability.

02

Repress retrotransposons

Reduce harmful retrotransposon activity associated with loss of oocyte function and DNA integrity in aged cells.

03

Improve cell quality

Measure whether treated oocytes show improved maturation rates and downstream fertilization potential versus controls.

04

Fit existing IVF

Supplement the in vitro maturation culture medium with EpicOva, without disrupting the established clinical chain.
